Privacy notice / 隐私说明

Experimental test site · 技术测试站点 — last updated November 13, 2026. This is not a complete commercial service or scientific personality analysis.

Camera and image processing / 摄像头与照片处理

Camera permission is requested only when you tap the camera button. As an optional fallback, you can select a local image file without sending it to a server. Camera frames and selected files are processed locally in your browser using MediaPipe Face Landmarker. We do not provide a face-photo upload endpoint, user account, or server-side face-photo database. Image frames, landmarks and report data are not deliberately uploaded by the Faceface application. You may choose to save or share a text report using your own device.

只有主动点击按钮才会申请摄像头权限。如果摄像头不可用，也可主动选择本地照片作为备用入口，文件不会上传服务器。面部照片在浏览器内使用 MediaPipe 本地分析。Faceface 当前没有照片上传接口、用户账号或服务端照片数据库；本应用不会主动上传照片、面部关键点或报告。只有你主动选择保存或分享时，文字报告才会交给设备上的下载或分享功能。

External requests / 第三方网络请求

The test site is served by GitHub Pages; loading it may disclose ordinary connection details such as your IP address to the hosting service. MediaPipe runtime files are requested from jsDelivr and the model is downloaded from Google-hosted storage. These services can receive network metadata. We have not fully audited all SDK telemetry or providers' retention behavior; do not interpret “local inference” as a guarantee of absolutely no third-party data processing.

测试站点由 GitHub Pages 提供网页服务，网站请求可能将 IP 等连接信息交给托管商。模型运行文件来自 jsDelivr，模型权重从 Google 托管服务下载。相关服务可能处理网络元数据；SDK 遥测及第三方保留政策尚待完整审查，因此“本地分析”不等于没有任何第三方网络信息处理。

Storage, advertising and analytics / 存储、广告与分析

Faceface does not intentionally write your photo, landmarks or reports to localStorage, sessionStorage or an application database. Clearing or leaving the page discards the in-memory photo and report, except a text file you explicitly downloaded or shared. We do not currently include advertising or product analytics scripts. A browser or service provider may maintain its normal cache and access logs.

本应用不主动把照片、关键点或报告写入浏览器持久化存储或数据库。清除/离开页面会丢弃应用内的临时内容，用户主动保存到设备的文件除外。当前不接广告或产品分析脚本；浏览器缓存及托管商访问日志不在应用控制范围内。

Content limitation / 内容限制

Face-reading references are historical and cultural. Appearance does not scientifically establish a person's character, health or fate. Any geometric labels are early experimental classifications, not population-calibrated measurements.

面相材料仅作历史文化介绍，不代表外貌能够科学预测人格、健康或命运。当前几何分类阈值为未完成多设备与人群标定的实验值。
