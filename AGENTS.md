# Faceface execution protocol

- Start each new session with ACTIVE_HANDOFF.md only (plus this file). Read targeted code or evidence only as required by the current task. Do not routinely load README, full STATUS, historical chats, OCR corpora, or old test logs.
- Goal: ship a free international mobile-first traditional face-reading cultural experience with manual camera capture, observable morphology, concise default and detailed source-linked reports. Traditional interpretations are cultural narratives, not factual deductions about a person's character, past or future.
- Preserve approved camera interaction and privacy constraints. No upload or retention of facial data by default. Do not fabricate sources, verification or personal predictions.
- On “continue”, choose the highest-value unblocked task against the goal. Avoid repeated extraction of known sources, redundant audits and unnecessary intermediate artifacts.
- Execute coherent batches. Store detailed evidence in existing relevant files; update ACTIVE_HANDOFF.md in place after each substantive batch. Keep it under 60 lines and preferably under 1200 Chinese characters. Do not append a running history.
- Chat output: result, evidence link, blocker if any, next task. Do not duplicate repository logs. Read historical decisions only when a concrete conflict arises.
- If current evidence contradicts the handoff, verify the targeted source and correct the handoff. Preserve history in Git; never claim work or tests not performed.
